Research & Publications

Information, technology and society.

My research examines how people seek, evaluate and use information in digital environments, with particular attention to health information, digital inequality and the social consequences of technological change. A parallel strand of my work focuses on media representations and public discourse.

Doctoral Research

PhD · Information Society Technologies · Tallinn University · 2026

Patterns of Online Health Information-Seeking Behaviour and Related Factors Among Estonian Older Adults

The dissertation examines how older adults in Estonia seek, evaluate and use health information online, and why access to digital services does not automatically produce equal benefits. It brings together questions of information behaviour, digital skills, socioeconomic resources, technology use and health-related decision-making.
