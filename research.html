Research & Publications

Information, technology, media and society.

My research spans two long-term areas: health information behaviour and digital inequality, and the ways drug-related issues are represented, debated and transformed through media and public discourse. Across both areas, I am interested in how information environments shape understanding, behaviour and policy.

Health Information Behaviour & Digital Inequality

This strand of my research examines how people seek, evaluate and use health information, how digital skills and socioeconomic resources shape those practices, and why access to digital services does not automatically lead to equal benefits.
